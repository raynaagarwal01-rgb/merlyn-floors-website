INCEPTION

Driven by a passion for design, quality and innovation, our journey began in 2002 with a dream to bring world-class flooring solutions to India's evolving architectural landscape.
Almost 25 years of passion and dedication have transformed that dream into reality, establishing us as a trusted name in the flooring industry. Today we proudly partner with leading architects, interior designers, builders and distributors across the country to deliver premium flooring solutions.

OUR JOURNEY

Over the years we have evolved by consistently understanding the changing needs and preferences of the market, enabling us to deliver relevant and forward-thinking flooring solutions.
Over time we have continuously expanded our product portfolio while strengthening our presence across the country.
As our business evolved, we expanded beyond supplying flooring products by establishing our own rubber tile manufacturing facility.
Today, with a diverse portfolio of innovative flooring materials and a strong Pan-India presence, we continue to create spaces that are as functional as they are visually compelling.

VISION

To be India's most trusted and innovative flooring solutions company, inspiring exceptional spaces through quality, design, and craftsmanship.

MISSION

To deliver premium flooring solutions that combine aesthetics, durability, and sustainability while providing unmatched customer service and long-term value.
